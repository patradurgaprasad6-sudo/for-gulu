# 💗 A Little World for Gulu

*Not a big gift. Just a small place on the internet, made with a very full heart.*

---

### Hey, Gulu! 🌷

If you've found your way here, it means you've discovered a tiny surprise someone made just for you.

You might think your reels, your thoughtful presents, your random messages, and all those little efforts are ordinary things. But they're not ordinary to me. They have a way of turning a regular day into a better one.

I wanted to make something that could say **thank you** in a way that a simple message never quite could.

### A few moments I wanted to keep close 📸

- 🥤 **Two shakes, one memory.** A small stop that became a photo worth saving.
- 😂 **The ride back from Rourkela.** You resting your leg on me, both of us talking, laughing, and remembering funny incidents. I don't know if you remember every detail, but I remember how happy that journey felt.
- 🥹 **That little feeding moment.** Maybe it was just a simple gesture to you. To me, it became one of those small memories that stays.
- 🎮 **The random moments.** The photos, the games, the jokes, and all the silly things that make a friendship feel like its own little world.

### One thing I hope you remember 💌

You don't have to keep doing thoughtful things to earn appreciation. You deserve kindness, happiness, and people who show up for you simply because you're you.

I may not always be able to match every lovely thing you do, and I may not always find the perfect words. But I notice. I remember. And I am genuinely grateful to have you as my best friend.

I hope this little website makes you smile. And if it makes you emotional, please remember: those are happy tears, okay? 😭💗

---

**Made with friendship, gratitude, and a whole lot of affection.**

### For Gulu, always. ♡
